Welcome to the Abyss, MC..

You wake up in the mysterious Abyss with no idea how you got there. After meeting Leo and Yuna, you join them on a journey to find a way back home while uncovering the secrets of this strange world. As you travel through the Abyss, you encounter dangerous creatures, unfamiliar places, and clues about its mysterious past. Along the way, you begin to realize that your arrival may not have been an accident. Together, you must survive the Abyss and discover the truth behind why you were brought there in the first place. Who knows..could be something good or bad.
